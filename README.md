Bugay Dagat  
An Online Platform for Seafood Delivery

Overview
Bugay Dagat is an online seafood delivery platform designed to connect customers with fresh and high-quality seafood products directly from local fishermen and seafood suppliers. The system provides a fast, reliable, and convenient way for customers to browse, order, and receive seafood products online.

The platform aims to:
- Support local seafood businesses and fishermen
- Provide customers with fresh seafood at affordable prices
- Simplify seafood ordering and delivery processes
- Ensure secure and efficient online transactions
